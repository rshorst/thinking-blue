Thinking Blue: Discreteness, Atmosphere, and Digital Mediation

This inquiry begins in thinking blue and seeks to find blue resonances across atmosphere, language, memory, and digital mediations. Blue appears in sky and water as gradients of light and depth—continuous phenomena produced through atmospheric scattering and relational perception. The inquiry thinks planetary blue together with digital and posthuman theory, tracing how blue becomes abstracted, permuted, and discretized as it moves and becomes digital. This project seeks to follow the continuous phenomena of blue and identify multimodal flight patterns across different registers: planetary blue; languaged blue; #0000ff; memory blue, blue stored in dreams and digital architectures, invisible blue, and sounded blue.

Thinking blue is both method and matter. Through fictopoeisis, a form of speculative theory writing, and arts-based digital inquiry, this project explores how the discreteness of blues moves across perception, language, and digital mediation shaping how skies and blue guitars might come to be.
